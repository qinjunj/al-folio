I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/) (UIUC), advised by [Prof. Sarita V. Adve](http://sadve.cs.illinois.edu/). Before joining UIUC, I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://www.wisc.edu).

## 💡 Research Interests

Distributed XR systems, networked immersive systems, XR application-runtime-network co-design, user-perceived quality.

## 🏅 Awards and Honors

- **Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship 2025 — Finalist**, “Joint Runtime and Transport Protocol Design for Distributed XR”
- **Saburo Muroga Endowed Fellowship**, 2021–2022, UIUC
- **Trewartha Senior Honors Thesis Research Award**, Spring 2021, UW-Madison, advised by [Prof. Matt Sinclair](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~sinclair/)
- **Dean’s List** for 5 semesters, UW-Madison
